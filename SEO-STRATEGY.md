# SEO strategy — Georgian market (October 2026)

## Competitors in search
- **Georgian results for "ბუღალტრული მომსახურება / ფასები":** cba.ge and kudos.ge (dedicated price pages), plus the marketplaces
  servisebi.ge, ss.ge and momsaxureba.ge (freelance accountants), and legal.ge.
- **English results for "accounting services Georgia / Tbilisi":** pbservices.ge, tpsolution.ge, bookkeeping.ge, ECOVIS Georgia,
  readymade.ge and gegidze.com. These target foreigners: LLC accounting, individual entrepreneur / 1% small business status,
  company registration.
- **Price pages rank:** competitors with published prices rank for "ფასი / ფასები". Outsourcify publishes concrete prices,
  and the pricing page title and description now lead with them.

## Done on the site
- 5 new service pages, each with FAQ schema and Service schema, linked from the menu, footer, homepage tabs and sitemap.
- Titles and descriptions rewritten around the queries above, including "თბილისში", "ფასები 2026", "შპს" and "ინდმეწარმე".
- 4 new FAQ entries for question-style searches (prices, what the monthly service includes, entrepreneurs, restoring books).
- Technical: hreflang, canonical, Organization/AccountingService with address and hours, OfferCatalog prices, image sitemap.
  Lighthouse SEO scores 100.

## Next steps (off-site, owner's action)
1. **Google Business Profile:** same name, address and phone as the site, category "Accounting firm"; ask clients for reviews.
2. **Listings:** servisebi.ge, ss.ge services, momsaxureba.ge, yell.ge. Each listing links back to outsourcify.ge (this is where competitors get traffic).
3. **Content:** a monthly article answering a real client question (e.g. declaration deadlines, small business status limits,
   payroll changes). Check every figure against rs.ge before publishing.
4. **Search Console:** submit the sitemap, watch "Queries", and update titles for pages with high impressions and low CTR.
5. Add real service titles (roles) for team members, and LinkedIn links where available.
